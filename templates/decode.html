TanzoGlyph Character Guide

TanzoGlyph uses different Unicode character sets to represent personality components:

  • Traits (Latin A-Z): AaBbCcDdEe - Maps to personality traits like Openness and Creativity
  • Archetypes (Cyrillic): АБВГДЕЖЗ - Represents character archetypes like Creator or Explorer
  • Spiritual Arcs (Greek): ΑΒΓΔΕΖΗ - Encodes developmental journeys like Awakening or Transformation
  • Mood (Half-width Kana): ｱｲｳｴｵｶｷｸｹｺ - Represents emotional states
  • Scars (Braille): ⠁⠃⠇⠏⠟⠋⠓⠊⠥⠵ - Encodes psychological traumas or challenges
  • Projection (Block Drawing): █▓▒░▀▄▌▐ - Represents style and presence attributes
  • Intensity (Geometric Shapes): ○◔◑◕●◆■▲◉◎◌ - Encodes numeric intensity values from 0.0 to 1.0
  • Inflection (Arrows): ⇑↑↗→↘↓⇓↔⤊⤋⇥⇤ - Represents patterns of change (rising, falling, steady)
