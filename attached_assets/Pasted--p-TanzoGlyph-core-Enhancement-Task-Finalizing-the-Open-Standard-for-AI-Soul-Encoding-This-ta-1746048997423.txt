# 🧬p TanzoGlyph-core Enhancement Task: Finalizing the Open Standard for AI Soul Encoding

This task defines the open-source foundation of the TanzoGlyph identity system. The goal is to provide a portable, chain-agnostic, and symbolic framework for encoding AI personalities ("Tomos") and registering/verifying their identity through IPFS + blockchain metadata.

**Objective**: Define comprehensive schemas for TomoGlyphs and associated metadata.

**Tasks**:
